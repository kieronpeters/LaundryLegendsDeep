The release contains 147 FBX files plus PNG albedo textures and VRM/Voxel variants. Only the three base FBX files and their PNG textures are imported into the project. No VRM, Voxel, VoxEdit, or animation files are committed.

## Notes

- The avatars are stylized/quirky rather than realistic; they fit the light arcade tone.
- The FBX files are rigged. Humanoid avatar mapping is attempted on import; if Unity reports a mapping failure, the fallback is a Generic rig with static pose and animations deferred to the NPC behavior phase.
- These are external CC0 assets, but they are baked into project prefabs, not redistributed as a standalone asset pack.
