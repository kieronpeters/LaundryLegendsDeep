## Verdict for Laundry Legends

Downloaded and inspected as a comparison candidate only. **Not recommended**
for the laundromat NPCs: the characters are chibi-proportioned fantasy
adventurers (huge head, short limbs, tunics and crossbows). The rig is good and
the pack is CC0, but the art direction does not fit a modern laundromat.
Details in `ArtSource/Notes/character-candidates.md`.
