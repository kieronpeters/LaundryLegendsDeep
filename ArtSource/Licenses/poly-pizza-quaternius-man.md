## Provenance and licence verification

Poly Pizza is blocked only for its search API (which needs a key); the model
page and the `static.poly.pizza` asset CDN are reachable without login and the
page states "No login required". The page metadata read on 2026-11-01:

```
Creator:  Quaternius
Tags:     Low Poly, People & Characters, Animated, #Person, #Man, #Human, #character
Created:  Aug 24, 2021
Formats:  FBX / GLTF
Licence:  Public Domain (CC0)
```

Two independent checks agree on CC0: the Poly Pizza model page, and the
author's own pack pages (quaternius.com), which mark their packs CC0 and say
"Free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects". No
non-commercial, no-derivatives, or "research only" term appears in either.

The clip set (`Man_Idle`, `Man_Walk`, `Man_Run`, `Man_Standing`, `Man_Sitting`,
`Man_Jump`, `Man_RunningJump`, `Man_Punch`, `Man_Clapping`, `Man_Death`,
`Man_SwordSlash`) matches Quaternius' 2021 "Animated Men Pack". The download was
taken from the author's Poly Pizza upload rather than itch.io because the
itch.io checkout flow is not usable in this environment. The asset is CC0 in
both places, so the redistribution channel does not change the terms.
