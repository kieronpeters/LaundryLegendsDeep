## Why this task exists

The owner's Phase 3 walkthrough found that the basket could be picked up but not put down. `CarryController.TryDrop` existed and was tested, but no input called it, and the prompt view offered no drop affordance. That blocked the acceptance item "Player can pick up, carry, and place a basket and loose laundry."

## Behavior implemented

- Holding an item and pressing `Q` calls the existing wall-aware `TryDrop`.
- Input is ignored while `Time.timeScale == 0`.
- `DropKey` and `DropKeyLabel` are exposed; `Q` is serialized on the Player prefab rather than relying on a field initializer.
- The prompt view shows `[Q] Drop` while carrying and hides it when empty-handed or paused.
- The prompt view's `_carry` is wired by setup and checked by `ValidatePlayerSetup`.
