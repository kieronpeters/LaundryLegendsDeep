## 1. Objective and what shipped

Complete the first playable laundry loop: a customer arrives, hands over an order, the
order is laundered through the real staff pipeline, the customer waits, collects, pays
exactly once, and leaves — repeatable without console commands or a scene reload.

Confirmed shipped, as of `81f5b1c`:

- `LaundryOrder` state model with a fixed price captured at creation and a monotonic stage mirror.
- `OrderLedger` with an exactly-once settlement guard keyed by order id (paid *and* abandoned orders are settled and immutable).
- `GameState.TrySpend` / `TryCredit` / `CanCredit`; no public money setter.
- `CustomerAgent` lifecycle `Arrive → Queue → HandOff → Waiting → Collect → Pay → Leave`, reusing the existing lane waypoints and `CarrySocket`.
- Customer lane waypoints built into `Laundromat.unity`; the spawner respawns after the previous customer leaves.
- `OrderTicketView` (IMGUI placeholder, matching the existing prompt style).
- Patience timer with an abandon path, and a fast-service tip.
- Demo `PickupCustomer` disabled so the real customer owns the wrapped bundle.

Placeholder tuning values currently baked as serialized constants (**proposed, untuned**):
order price 40, tip 10, patience 90 s, tip window 60 s, respawn delay 4 s. These are
implementation defaults, not balanced economy numbers; Phase 6/14 own real tuning.

## 3. What was fixed during the phase

- **Character rig hierarchy.** Upper-leg bones were parented under `Body`, and the feet under `Bone`, so the clothed character could not pose correctly. The hierarchy was corrected, which is what made a presentable customer possible (lead-reported fix in `3498067`; the visual result was not independently re-inspected by this report's author).
- **Magenta material.** The imported character rendered with Unity's missing-material magenta shader. The material assignment was corrected; the character now renders with its intended material. Root cause detail is **unverified** here.
- **Faceless mannequin replaced.** `3498067` shipped a clothed NPC with a Unity humanoid avatar, ending the Phase 4 "tinted mannequin" debt for the customer role.
- **Order theft by the demo actor.** The Phase 4 demo `PickupCustomer` could take the wrapped bundle before the real customer collected it, breaking the loop. It is now disabled (`f4bb0ef`).
- **End-to-end test was faking progress.** Before `f4bb0ef`, `CustomerWalksInWaitsPaysOnceAndLeaves` forced item stages. It now drives the real washer/dryer/wrap station, which is why it takes 11.2 s and is the strongest single piece of evidence for the phase.
- **Payment duplication hardening.** The exactly-once guard lives in `OrderLedger`, not in UI or the agent, per the plan's critical-defect rule.

## 5. Open items

### Blocks Phase 5 closure

1. **Human playtest of the loop** (criterion 5): clarity of the ticket, whether the wait reads,
   whether payment is legible, and whether one order feels satisfying. Nothing in a green unit
   suite answers this.
2. **Capture set** for the loop (customer at counter, ticket mid-wash, ready bundle, payment,
   customer leaving), per plan `LL-P5-008`.

### Polish (does not block closure, should be scheduled)

- **Multi-customer queue spots** (`LL-P5-007`): the phase ships a single customer. Real queue
  slots are what make "repeatable" feel like a business rather than a queue of one.
- **Abandoned-order recovery**: an abandoned order is dead money. Either keep the wrapped
  bundle payable on a later visit, add a "ready shelf", or explicitly design it as a loss —
  and write that into `GAME_DESIGN.md`.
- **No test for automatic respawn**: add one test that lets the spawner respawn and completes a
  *second* order, so "repeat the loop" is evidenced rather than inferred.
- **Tip model** is a flat constant with one time window; `GAME_DESIGN.md` proposes `$0-$5` by
  speed/care.
- **No player-facing payment screen**; payment happens implicitly. `GAME_DESIGN.md` calls for
  coins/cash feedback and a money HUD update.
- **Carried-over Phase 4 visual debt, still open:** wrapped bundle renders opaque pale, carried
  bundle floats instead of being gripped, room lighting flat with a dark ceiling.
- **Voice naturalness** has never been reviewed by a human ear.
- `PROJECT_STATE.md` and `.orchestrator/state.json` still describe Phase 5 as in progress and
  were not updated by this report (out of scope for a single-file documentation task).

## 6. What a human must still check

1. **Clarity:** can a first-time player tell what the ticket is saying, what stage the laundry is
   at, and when they get paid — without being told?
2. **Fun and pacing:** is the ~90 s patience window right, does the wait feel like anticipation
   or dead time, does the 11 s automatic pipeline feel visible enough to watch?
3. **Payment legibility:** does the player see money arrive, and does the exactly-once behavior
   feel correct rather than arbitrary?
4. **Abandonment:** does an impatient customer leaving read as a fair loss or as a bug?
5. **Audio:** the customer greeting/thanks and payment sounds have never been reviewed for
   naturalness, level, or timing.
6. **Visual acceptance:** the three Phase 4 art debts above, as they appear in this phase.

Until items 1–3 are answered by a person, Phase 5 is **test-green, not playable-verified**, and
Phase 6 should not be declared to depend on closed Phase 5 acceptance without a recorded human
sign-off.
