## Behavior implemented

- Identity: `TemplateId` is authored per prefab; `InstanceId` is generated once per instance and is intentionally not serialized until the save phase.
- Kinds: `Basket`, `LooseLaundry`, and `Bag`. Only `LooseLaundry` reports `IsWasherLoadable`.
- Pickup: rejected when already holding, inactive, or not carryable. A successful pickup parents the item to `HoldAnchor`, disables all child colliders, makes the rigidbody kinematic, and clears its velocity.
- Drop: restores the original collider-enabled states and rigidbody mode/gravity, detaches to world space, and places the item at a wall-aware point in front of the player. A blocked line shortens the drop distance instead of placing the item inside geometry.
- Placement: `CarrySlot` accepts only one carryable item of an accepted kind. Occupied, incompatible, or non-carryable items are rejected without releasing the held item. Slots stabilize the occupant's rigidbody and restore it on `Take`.
- No-stuck: double pickup/drop is rejected; a destroyed held item clears carry state; a disabled held item is dropped; disabling `CarryController` drops the item; scene unload clears state without touching destroyed objects.
- Player setup: `SetupPlayerCarry` is idempotent, reuses an existing `HoldAnchor`, rewires `_holdAnchor`, and saves the prefab. `ValidatePlayerSetup` now requires `CarryController` and `HoldAnchor`.

## Known risks and limitations

- `InstanceId` is not persisted; the save phase must serialize it or identity will not survive a reload.
- Drop placement is a simple forward ray, so low obstacles and uneven floors are not sampled. It prevents wall insertion but is not a full placement solver.
- `CarrySlot` does not validate that the item is not already in another slot; Phase 3 assumes one owner at a time.
- Held-item scaling is inherited from the anchor (`localScale = 1` is not applied); prefab authoring must keep item scale sane.
- The hold anchor is a fixed placeholder offset and has not been reviewed for visual comfort.
