## Flake found and fixed

A PlayMode run failed in `PlayerMovesAndLooks` with `Forward input should move the player forward.` The test called `Move` once and expected more than 0.005 m. Batch PlayMode runs at an uncapped frame rate, so a single frame can be shorter than 1.25 ms and produce less movement. The test now applies forward input for 0.1 seconds of accumulated `Time.deltaTime` and expects more than 0.05 m. Two subsequent PlayMode runs passed 8/8.

This was a test-timing defect, not a movement-controller defect.
