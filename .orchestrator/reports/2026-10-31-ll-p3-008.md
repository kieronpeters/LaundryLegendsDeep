## Behavior implemented

- `LaundryItemInteractable` implements `IInteractable` and requires a `LaundryItem`.
- Prompt is kind-specific: `Pick up basket`, `Pick up laundry`, or `Pick up bag`.
- Eligible when enabled, carryable, active, not parented under a `CarryController`, not parented under a `CarrySlot`, and the interactor has a `CarryController`.
- `Interact` calls `CarryController.TryPickUp`. When the player already holds an item, the pickup fails and the held item is unchanged; a second item cannot be stolen.
- An item held by the player or loaded into the washer slot does not report pickup eligibility.
- `SetupItemPickup` adds or reuses exactly one component per item prefab and rewires `_item`; `BuildItems` runs it automatically.
- `ValidateItems` now fails when a prefab or scene instance lacks a pickup component or its item reference is wrong.
- `PlayerInteractor.IsAlive` now uses `ReferenceEquals` so a destroyed Unity interactable is treated as dead.

## Known risks and limitations

- The pickup prompt says `Pick up ...` even when the player's hands are full; the interaction no-ops. There is no "hands full" prompt.
- `LaundryItemInteractable` uses `GetComponentInParent<CarryController>` and `<CarrySlot>` checks, which assume the item stays under those parents while held or loaded.
- The pickup component is a separate component rather than part of `LaundryItem`; moving an item prefab without it will fail `ValidateItems`.
- `PlayerInteractor.IsAlive` now matches the corrected check in `InteractionPromptView`; the duplicated helper could be consolidated later.
- The item colliders remain physical while dropped, so a dropped item can still roll or be nudged; no placement solver exists yet.
