## Why an editor tool

Scenes are complex serialized YAML. Creating them through the Unity API, rather than hand-editing YAML, produces valid files and makes the setup repeatable. The tool is idempotent: it keeps existing scenes and only adds missing ones, so later content is not overwritten.

The tool exposes two menu items:

- `Laundry Legends/Setup Foundation Scenes`
- `Laundry Legends/Validate Foundation Scenes`

It also provides static methods for batch mode:

- `LaundryLegends.Editor.SceneSetupTool.CreateFoundationScenes`
- `LaundryLegends.Editor.SceneSetupTool.ValidateFoundationScenes`

## Scene contents

The three scenes are intentionally empty shells (`NewSceneSetup.EmptyScene`). No camera, light, player, UI, or greybox content exists yet; those arrive in `LL-P1-005` through `LL-P1-007`. Empty scenes open without missing scripts and give later tasks a clean base.
