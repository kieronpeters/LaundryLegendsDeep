## What changed

### Order loop and economy
- Customer counter waypoint moved out of the counter collider; customers route via a room-centre waypoint and exit via a doorway waypoint.
- Staff drop-off stand distance raised to 1.6 m and idle point moved to (-4.6, 0, -0.9); both sat inside the counter shell, which pinned the staff and stalled every order.
- `LaundryStaff.WalkTo` now has a 20 s per-leg timeout so a blocked station can never freeze the shop.
- Fold-table stand distance raised to 2 m (the 1.4 m point sat inside the table).

### Hidden back room
- `HiddenRoomSetupTool` now measures the shop from its **floor** renderer, not every renderer; the frontage/street had pushed the room to x=10.2 while the door is at x=8.2.
- Room is 8x8 m at x=8.2, centred on the door; the west-wall gap is centred on the door leaf (`SecretDoor_Foreshadowed`), not the shelf group root (which sits at the origin).
- Shop east wall is cut 1.6 m wide at the doorway (`Wall_East` split into south/north/header) and a `BackRoomThreshold` bridges the floor seam.
- Ceiling uses the laundry ceiling material; floor uses a generated **black casino carpet** (gold diamond lattice, red centres, 6x6 tiling).
- Furnishing: bar counter, potted plant, two warm sconces, and two original wordplay posters (`STREET FOLDERS II`, `SPIN CYCLE SHOWDOWN`) generated by `tools/make-posters.ps1`.
- Arcade cabinet: import rotation is preserved from the **prefab asset** and the cabinet is grounded, so it stands upright with the screen facing the door. Table and chairs removed per owner.

### Characters (source fix)
- Blender 4.5.15 was already installed; the MakeHuman blocker was a missing 55-byte `settings.ini`.
- Patched `makehuman_npc_plugin.py` to apply each garment's `deleteVerts` vertex mask (the clothes chooser's own logic), so the naked body is no longer exported under the clothes.
- Re-exported `Customer_Human` (female_casualsuit01: tee + full jeans) and `Attendant_Human` (male_casualsuit06); imported the FBX and textures, switched `CharacterAppearanceFixTool` from `casualsuit02` to `casualsuit01`.
- Removed the mesh-inflation postprocessor and the placeholder first-person hands; hid 13 clutter/placeholder objects (dirt decals, clue cubes, build placeholders).

### HUD
