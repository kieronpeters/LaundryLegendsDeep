## Behavior implemented

- `IInteractable` exposes `InteractionPrompt`, `CanInteract(GameObject)`, and `Interact(GameObject)`.
- `SimpleInteractable` remains eligible by default and stays compatible with the existing interaction test. It becomes ineligible when the serialized flag is cleared or the component is disabled. `Interact` raises its event only when eligible.
- `PlayerInteractor` reports a target only when the hit collider resolves to an eligible `IInteractable` within range and time is running.
- A non-interactable collider blocks the ray; targets behind it are not reported.
- `TryInteract()` re-resolves and dispatches, returning `true` only when an eligible interaction ran. It respects pause.
- `TargetChanged` fires when the target or prompt appears, changes, or clears.
- `InteractKey` and `InteractKeyLabel` are the single source for input display; no current UI hard-codes the key.

## Known risks and limitations

- `TargetChanged` reports eligible targets only. An ineligible prop is indistinguishable from empty space at the interactor level; future UI that needs to explain why an action is unavailable must add that state deliberately.
- The interactor re-raycasts on `TryInteract`, which is acceptable at this scale but may need caching if input and tests call it every frame.
- The interaction key remains a serialized `KeyCode`; rebinding and gamepad support are out of scope.
- Carry cleanup for a destroyed/disabled target is not implemented yet and belongs to `LL-P3-003`.
