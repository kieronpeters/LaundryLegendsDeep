## What was added

### Editor validation

`Phase2SetupTool.ValidatePublicNavigation` opens `Laundromat.unity`, syncs transforms, and checks:

- Seven linecast routes: entrance-to-counter, central aisle, machine aisle, east circulation, waiting-to-central, central-to-entrance, and counter-to-entrance.
- Critical colliders on the counter, folding table, machine, waiting chairs/table, storage shelves, utility divider/cabinet, service panel, and supply shelf.
- Non-null renderer materials for every renderer under `GreyboxRoom`.
- No missing `MonoBehaviour` references.
- Camera spheres of radius 0.2 m at 1.5 m and 1.7 m at eight representative waypoints.

Failures report the first blocking collider and distance. The command exits nonzero on any failure.

### PlayMode coverage

`PublicNavigationAndCameraClearanceAreValid` repeats the route linecasts, critical-object collider checks, renderer-material checks, and camera-sphere samples in the live scene. The PlayMode suite now has 12 tests.
