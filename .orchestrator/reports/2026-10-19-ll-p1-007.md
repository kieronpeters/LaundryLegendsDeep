## Design

### Room

- Interior 16 m wide (X), 12 m deep (Z), 3.2 m high.
- Floor is a 0.2 m thick cube with the top at `y = 0`; a matching ceiling closes the room.
- Walls are 0.2 m thick boxes. The south wall has a 4 m wide, 2.4 m high entrance opening with a header.
- Counter at `(-4.5, 0.55, -2.5)`, 3.2 m wide, 1.1 m high, with a metal top.
- Folding table at `(4.5, 0.5, 1.0)`, 3.2 m wide, 1.0 m high, with a metal top.
- Machine wall on the north side: a metal back panel and eight 0.8 x 1.1 x 0.7 m washer/dryer boxes, alternating blue and tan materials, with dark cylindrical door fronts.
- Secret door on the east wall at `z = 3`: a dark 1.2 x 2.2 m panel in a metal frame with a handle. Its collider stays enabled; it is visual foreshadowing only and has no interaction.

### Lighting

- Four ceiling point lights at `y = 2.9`, range 12 m, intensity 2.4, warm white.
- Two of the point lights cast soft shadows; two do not.
- Directional fill light at intensity 0.55.
- Scene uses flat ambient light `(0.24, 0.25, 0.28)` and fog disabled.
- Lighting was tuned from the first capture: low intensity left the room too dark; 3.2 clipped the walls toward white; 2.4 with a slightly darker wall material keeps surfaces readable.

### Reproducibility

- `GreyboxSetupTool.BuildGreyboxRoom` creates or updates the seven Standard materials, rebuilds the prefab from primitives, removes old room/temporary-floor instances, places the room, positions the player, configures lighting, and saves the scene.
- `GreyboxSetupTool.ValidateGreyboxRoom` checks floor support at eight points, five wall/obstacle raycasts, the secret-door collider, point-light count, and player spawn height.
- `GreyboxSetupTool.CaptureGreyboxPreview` renders entrance and secret-door views to PNG under `TestResults/` for review.

## Retry and repair

- The first traversal test used a fixed 200-frame loop. In batch PlayMode the editor runs at an uncapped frame rate, so 200 frames represented only about 0.55 m of movement and the assertion failed. The test now accumulates 2.6 seconds of `Time.deltaTime` while applying forward input. The retry passed.
- The first lighting capture was too dark at intensity 1.8. A second capture at 3.2 over-brightened the walls. The final value is 2.4 with wall color `(0.55, 0.57, 0.60)`.

## Known risks and limitations

- The room is deliberately crude greybox geometry with flat colors and no textures.
- Machine wall, counter, folding table, and secret door are static; none are interactable.
- The secret door is only foreshadowed visually; no secret-room access logic exists.
- Point lights are real-time and unoptimized; performance is unmeasured.
- There is no navigation mesh, occlusion culling, reflection probe, or baked lighting.
- The player spawn and room dimensions may change when real props replace greybox geometry.
- `GreyboxSetupTool` rebuilds the room prefab; manual edits to the prefab would be overwritten by the tool.
