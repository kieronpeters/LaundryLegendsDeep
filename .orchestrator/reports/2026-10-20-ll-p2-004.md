## Risks and limitations

- The cover is deliberately readable as a service shelf; a human may find it too obvious or too subtle. `LL-P2-006` must judge this in motion.
- The shelf does not move or animate. Future reveal logic must decide whether it slides, rolls, or is removed.
- The service panel and frame gaps are static primitives and may not survive final art replacement without a new concealment design.
- The validation checks the shelf body's footprint and the first aisle-to-door collider; it does not prove every oblique sightline is concealed.
- Rendering captures require a graphics-capable batch run; `-nographics` is not safe for them.
- `BuildSecretConcealment` is additive and idempotent but must be rerun if `GreyboxSetupTool.BuildGreyboxRoom` rebuilds the prefab.
