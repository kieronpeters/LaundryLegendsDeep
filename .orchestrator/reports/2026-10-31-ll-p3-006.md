## Behavior implemented

- `MachineLoadHook` implements `IInteractable` and is attached to `Machine_1`, an existing washer.
- The hook owns one `CarrySlot` on an additive `DrumSlot` child, restricted to `LaundryItemKind.LooseLaundry`.
- Empty machine: prompt is `Load washer`; occupied: prompt is `Take laundry`.
- Load path: requires an empty slot and a carried item that `LaundryItem.IsWasherLoadable` accepts. A loose item moves into the slot; a basket or bag is rejected with `LastResult` naming the kind; an occupied machine rejects a second load; empty hands reject with `Nothing to load`.
- Unload path: requires an occupied slot and an empty-handed carrier. It takes the slot's occupant and gives that exact instance to `CarryController.TryPickUp`. Full hands reject without taking the item; a failed pickup re-places the item into the slot.
- Double-load cannot replace the occupant; double-unload returns `null` after the slot is empty.
- All rejections leave both the held item and slot unchanged and write a `LastResult` message plus one `Debug.Log`.
- `BuildMachineHook` reuses existing components, removes stray hooks on other machines, and can be rerun after a `GreyboxRoom` rebuild.
- `ValidateMachineHook` fails if the hook is missing, duplicated, on the wrong machine, wired to the wrong slot, accepts a non-loose kind, or if `Machine_1` moved/rescaled/lost its collider.
