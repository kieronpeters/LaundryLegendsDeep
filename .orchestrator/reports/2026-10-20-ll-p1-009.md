## Human verification checklist

1. Open Unity `6000.0.64f1` with this project.
2. Open `Assets/LaundryLegends/Scenes/Laundromat.unity`.
3. Enter Play mode at 1920x1080 or the target window size.
4. Walk with WASD and look with the mouse. Check speed, smoothness, camera height, and comfort.
5. Press Esc. Confirm time stops, the cursor becomes visible, and the Resume button works. Resume and confirm the cursor locks again.
6. Inspect the room: entrance, floor, walls, counter, folding table, machine wall, ceiling lights, and the closed dark secret door on the east wall.
7. Record: date, commit, resolution, pass/fail for movement/camera/cursor/greybox, and any issues.
8. If a check fails, create a scoped fix task, rerun the affected automated checks, and repeat this human check.

Do not mark Phase 1 complete until this evidence is recorded.
