## Design

### FirstPersonController

- `CharacterController`-based movement with gravity and grounded handling.
- Mouse look rotates the player yaw and clamps camera pitch to +/-85 degrees.
- Public `Move(Vector2)` and `Look(Vector2)` methods keep input reading in `Update` and let tests drive the controller.
- Reads only legacy `Input.GetAxis` / `GetAxisRaw`; no Input System package.

### PauseController

- Esc toggles pause, releases/locks the cursor, and sets `Time.timeScale` to 0/1.
- OnGUI overlay with a Resume button is an explicit placeholder until real UI exists.
- Cursor APIs are skipped in batch mode because headless runs do not expose a cursor; manual verification is required.

### Interaction

- `PlayerInteractor` raycasts from the assigned camera up to 3 m and reports `CurrentTarget` / `CurrentPrompt`.
- `IInteractable` is the prop contract; `SimpleInteractable` is the placeholder/test implementation.
- E calls `Interact`; interaction is skipped while paused.

### Player prefab and scene

- Root: CharacterController, FirstPersonController, PlayerInteractor, PauseController.
- Child `PlayerCamera`: Camera (70 FOV, 0.05 near plane) and AudioListener.
- Laundromat contains the prefab at `(0, 0.1, 0)`, a 20x20 temporary plane floor, and a directional light. These are placeholders for `LL-P1-007`.
