## Design

### GameState

- Instance-based state: `Money` (read-only), `Day`, `TimeOfDayHours`, and progression flags.
- No static mutable money/order state.
- Money has **no public mutator**. `EconomyService` will be the single writer when transactions arrive, matching the architecture invariant.
- `SetFlag`/`HasFlag` and `AdvanceDay` are available for later services.

### GameSession

- A `MonoBehaviour` created once in the `Bootstrap` scene.
- `Awake` creates exactly one `GameState`, calls `DontDestroyOnLoad`, and destroys duplicate sessions.
- `GameSession.Instance` is a service reference only; gameplay state stays inside `State`.
- Lifetime: application start to quit; scene transitions do not recreate it. Documented in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

### Routing

- `SceneRouter` is the single owner of foundation scene names and transitions.
- `BootstrapController.Start` verifies that `GameState` initialized, logs the starting state, then loads `MainMenu`.
- `MainMenuController.StartGame` loads `Laundromat`; `Return` also triggers it until real UI exists.
