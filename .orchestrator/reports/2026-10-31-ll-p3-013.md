## Known risks and limitations

- The NPCs are static and stylized/quirky; the Sunflower and Cosmic avatars may not fit a realistic laundromat tone.
- The Customer mesh logged a self-intersecting-polygon warning during import; Unity discarded the affected polygon.
- Arm posing is a prefab override, not an animation; re-importing the FBX may reset it.
- The 14x importer scale is tuned to these avatars and must be rechecked if the source pack changes.
- No AnimatorController is assigned; behavior work must add one and retarget clips.
- The capture tool opens `Laundromat` and uses an additive preview scene; it does not save scene changes, but running it interactively can discard unsaved scene edits.
